# Cardano

A research-first proof-of-stake chain that extends Bitcoin's UTXO model to smart contracts (EUTXO) and pioneers on-chain governance. Each concept links back to the shared primitive it implements.

- [Cardano overview](overview.md) - Purpose, the research-first philosophy, the five eras, and the design in one view.
- [Ouroboros proof of stake](ouroboros.md) - Peer-reviewed PoS with liquid staking, no slashing, and no lock-up.
- [Extended UTXO (EUTXO)](eutxo.md) - Bitcoin's UTXO extended with datums and scripts: a third accounting model.
- [Native tokens](native-tokens.md) - A multi-asset ledger where tokens are first-class, minted without a contract.
- [Plutus and smart contracts](plutus.md) - Haskell-based validators in the EUTXO model, plus Marlowe and Aiken.
- [ADA monetary policy](monetary-policy.md) - A 45 billion cap funded from a diminishing reserve, with a treasury.
- [The hard fork combinator](hard-fork-combinator.md) - Upgrading via hard forks without a chain split.
- [Governance (Voltaire)](governance.md) - On-chain governance under CIP-1694: DReps, the committee, and the constitution.
- [Hard fork history](hard-forks.md) - Every activated fork from Byron (2017) to van Rossem (June 2026).
