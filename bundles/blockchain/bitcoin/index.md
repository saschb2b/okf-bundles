- [UTXO transactions](utxo-transactions.md) - Inputs, outputs, change, and implicit fees.
- [Bitcoin Script](script.md) - The intentionally limited, non-Turing-complete scripting language.
- [Upgrades: SegWit and Taproot](upgrades.md) - How Bitcoin evolves through conservative soft forks.
- [Lightning Network](lightning-network.md) - Off-chain payment channels for scaling.
