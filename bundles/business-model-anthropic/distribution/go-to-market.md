# Three ways Claude reaches buyers

| Channel | What it is |
|---------|------------|
| Direct | claude.ai (consumer, Pro, Max), the API console, and Claude Code |
| Cloud marketplaces | Claude resold through Amazon Bedrock, Google Vertex AI, and Microsoft Foundry (Azure) |
| Anthropic-operated cloud | "Claude Platform on AWS," billed via Claude Consumption Units [1] |

Over 100,000 customers run Claude on Amazon Bedrock as of April 2026. [2] The marketplace channels turn the hyperscalers into a distribution arm on top of being investors, which is the distinctive feature of Anthropic's go-to-market. These channels carry the [revenue streams](/revenue/revenue-streams.md) to the [customer segments](/market/segments.md).

# The hyperscaler partnerships are capital plus compute plus distribution

The same partners fund Anthropic, sell Claude, and supply the compute its [cost structure](/economics/cost-structure.md) depends on.

- **Amazon.** ~$8B invested cumulatively (stake reportedly worth over $70B). The 2026 expansion committed a further $5B, with up to ~$20B more discussed, plus up to 5 gigawatts of new compute. [3][4]
- **Google.** Roughly $3B invested historically for a ~14% stake, plus a reported additional ~$40B commitment and a Google/Broadcom partnership for 5 GW of next-generation TPU capacity. The $40B figure is press-reported, not fully broken out by Anthropic; treat it as estimated. [3][5]
- **Other compute channels** named in the Series H: SpaceX (Colossus GPU capacity), Micron, Samsung, and SK hynix. [5]

This entanglement is both an advantage and a dependency, and it is examined as part of the [moats](/moat/moats.md) and the [governance](/strategy/governance.md) trajectory.
