# The segments

| Segment | How they buy | Signal |
|---------|--------------|--------|
| Developers and startups | API console, direct integration | Core of the ~80% API revenue [1] |
| Enterprises | Enterprise seats, committed API spend | 300,000+ business customers; 1,000+ at >$1M/yr; eight of the Fortune 10 [2] |
| Prosumers / consumers | Claude Free / Pro / Max on claude.ai | Feeds the subscription line in [revenue streams](/revenue/revenue-streams.md) |
| Coding teams | Claude Code seats and usage | The standout; see below |

# Enterprise is where the money concentrates

Anthropic reports 300,000+ business customers, with over 1,000 spending more than $1M per year (up from 500+ months earlier) and the count of >$100K/year customers growing roughly 7x year over year. Eight of the Fortune 10 are customers. [2] This land-and-expand motion is the heart of the [enterprise-first bet](/strategy/key-bets.md), and it is sold through the channels in [go-to-market](/distribution/go-to-market.md).

Named enterprise customers include Block, Carvana, Indeed, Notion, Snowflake, eSentire, and Basis. [3]

# Coding is the standout segment

[Claude Code](/revenue/revenue-streams.md) production adopters named by Anthropic include Netflix, Microsoft, Uber, Mercado Libre, Shopify, Goldman Sachs, ServiceNow, Spotify, KPMG, L'Oreal, and Salesforce. Mercado Libre is targeting 90% autonomous coding across roughly 23,000 engineers by Q3 2026. [2][4] The strength here is what makes coding capability a [moat](/moat/moats.md), not just a feature.
