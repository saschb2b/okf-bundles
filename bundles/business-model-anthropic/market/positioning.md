# The claim

Anthropic positions itself as the frontier AI lab that treats safety as a product feature, not a tax. The framing is "transformative AI that helps people and society flourish," backed by Constitutional AI, published safety research, and a governance structure (see [governance](/strategy/governance.md)) built to keep the mission ahead of pure shareholder return.

# Why the positioning is commercial, not just ethical

For regulated and risk-averse buyers, the safety posture is a purchasing reason. It underwrites:

- Governed-AI enterprise partnerships (for example with Snowflake) and a HIPAA-ready enterprise tier. [1]
- The trust half of the [moats](/moat/moats.md): SSO, SCIM, audit logs, and a credible answer to "can we deploy this in a regulated workflow."
- Differentiation from rivals that lead on raw capability alone, letting Anthropic compete on trust where it cannot always win on price.

# The other half of positioning: coding capability

Alongside safety, Anthropic positions Claude as the best model for coding and agents, evidenced by its own claim that ~80% of its new production code is Claude-authored. [2] Capability and trust together are the pitch, and both feed the [key strategic bets](/strategy/key-bets.md).
