# Four bets

## 1. Enterprise-first monetization

Roughly 80% of revenue comes from API and enterprise usage, and the [customer base](/market/segments.md) is deepening: over 1,000 customers now spend more than $1M per year. [1] The bet is that metered enterprise consumption, not consumer subscriptions, is the durable engine, which is why it anchors the [revenue streams](/revenue/revenue-streams.md).

## 2. Coding and agents as the wedge

[Claude Code](/revenue/revenue-streams.md) (over $2.5B annualized run-rate) plus Claude Managed Agents (billed on tokens plus $0.08 per session-hour) position Claude as the default for autonomous software work. [1][2] Coding capability doubles as a [moat](/moat/moats.md), so winning here compounds.

## 3. Safety research as differentiation

Constitutional AI, the Anthropic Economic Index, and the PBC + Long-Term Benefit Trust [governance](/strategy/governance.md) are marketed as enterprise-trust advantages, the commercial expression of the [positioning](/market/positioning.md). [3]

## 4. Securing compute ahead of an IPO

Multi-gigawatt deals with Amazon (5 GW), Google/Broadcom (5 GW of TPUs), SpaceX, and memory partners aim to remove the capacity ceiling that constrains the [cost structure](/economics/cost-structure.md) and revenue growth. [4] With an IPO widely expected, locking in compute is the bet that lets the frontier-model flywheel keep turning.

## The model lineup that ties them together

A tiered lineup, Opus (max capability), Sonnet (production workhorse, now Sonnet 5), Haiku (cheap and fast), plus premium Fable and a Fast mode, lets customers price-match model to task under the [pricing model](/revenue/pricing.md). [4]
