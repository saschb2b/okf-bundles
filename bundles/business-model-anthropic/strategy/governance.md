# A public benefit corporation

Anthropic is a Delaware public benefit corporation. Its board carries a dual duty: the usual fiduciary duty to shareholders and a mission duty to "ensure transformative AI helps people and society flourish." [1] That mission duty is what the [safety-first positioning](/market/positioning.md) rests on legally rather than just rhetorically.

# The Long-Term Benefit Trust

A five-member Long-Term Benefit Trust (LTBT) of financially disinterested trustees elects a growing share of the board. Trust-appointed directors rise on fundraising milestones and reached a board majority in 2026 (roughly 1 to 2 of 5 by July 2026, 3 of 5 by November 2026). [2][3] The structure is designed so that mission governance strengthens, not weakens, as the company takes on more capital, and it is a distinct part of the [moats](/moat/moats.md).

# Funding trajectory

| Round | Amount | Post-money valuation | When |
|-------|--------|----------------------|------|
| Series G | $30B | $380B | early 2026 [4] |
| Series H | $65B | $965B | ~May 28, 2026 [5] |

The Series H was led by Altimeter, Dragoneer, Greenoaks, and Sequoia, with co-leads including Capital Group, Coatue, D1, GIC, ICONIQ, and XN, and included $15B previously committed by hyperscalers (see [go-to-market](/distribution/go-to-market.md)). It is widely framed as likely the last private round before an IPO. [5] Major investors include Amazon and Google as the largest strategic holders, alongside Sequoia, Lightspeed, General Catalyst, GIC, Temasek, Fidelity, T. Rowe Price, Coatue, and ICONIQ. [5]

The near-trillion-dollar valuation is what the [revenue run-rate](/revenue/revenue-streams.md) and the [margin-expansion story](/economics/cost-structure.md) are being priced against.
