# The five streams

| Stream | What it is | Weight |
|--------|------------|--------|
| Developer / enterprise API | Pay-per-token model access via the API console and direct integration | Largest, ~80% of revenue with enterprise usage [1][2] |
| Claude Code | Agentic coding tool, billed on usage and seats | ~$2.5B annualized run-rate by early 2026, >50% enterprise [3] |
| Consumer subscriptions | Claude Free / Pro / Max on claude.ai | Part of the ~20% non-API [1] |
| Team / Enterprise seats | Per-seat plans with SSO, admin, compliance | Part of the ~20% non-API [1] |
| Cloud-marketplace resale | Claude sold through Amazon Bedrock, Google Vertex AI, Microsoft Foundry | Metered, billed in Claude Consumption Units [4] |

The exact stream weights are directional. The 80%-from-API split has held since the ~$9B run-rate period in late 2025, so treat it as the shape of the business rather than a precise mid-2026 measurement. [1][2]

# API is the engine

Usage-based token billing to developers and enterprises is the core. Pricing is per-token and tiered by model, detailed in the [pricing model](/revenue/pricing.md). The buyers behind it are described in [customer segments](/market/segments.md), and the channels it flows through in [go-to-market](/distribution/go-to-market.md).

# Claude Code is the breakout

Generally available since May 2025, Claude Code reached ~$1B annualized by November 2025 and over $2.5B annualized run-rate by early 2026, more than doubling within 2026, with over half of that revenue from enterprises. [3] It is the clearest expression of the [coding-and-agents bet](/strategy/key-bets.md).

# Run-rate trajectory (contested)

- ~$9B run-rate at end of 2025, rising to ~$14B (Feb 2026). [5]
- ~$30B run-rate reported by Anthropic in April 2026; third-party trackers estimate higher (into the $40Bs) but those are not Anthropic disclosures. [6][2]

Treat anything above ~$30B as estimated. The scale of these figures is what the [funding and valuation](/strategy/governance.md) trajectory is priced against.
