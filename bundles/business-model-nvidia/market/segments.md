# The segments

| Segment | Who | Signal |
|---------|-----|--------|
| Hyperscalers / cloud | Microsoft, Amazon, Google, Meta, Oracle, plus GPU-clouds like CoreWeave | Dominant buyers, roughly half of Data Center revenue |
| Enterprise | Broad base via OEM systems and AI Enterprise software | Recurring-software expansion target |
| Sovereign AI / governments | Saudi Arabia (Humain), UAE (G42), Europe | Fast-growing, framed as multi-ten-billion; exact booked revenue not cleanly disclosed |
| Consumer (gamers) | GeForce | ~$16B FY2026 |
| Automotive & robotics | Drive, Isaac, Jetson Thor | ~$2.35B FY2026, small but strategically emphasized |

The hyperscaler demand is what drives the Data Center line in the [revenue streams](/revenue/revenue-streams.md), and sovereign AI is one of the [key strategic bets](/strategy/key-bets.md).

# Concentration is the standing risk

In Q3 FY2026, four direct customers each exceeded 10% of total revenue: Customer A at 22%, B at 15%, C at 13%, and D at 11%, together about 61% of revenue. [1] A nuance that cuts both ways: these "direct customers" are typically OEMs, ODMs, and distributors rather than the end hyperscalers, so the reported concentration both understates the true end-demand concentration and overstates any single buyer's leverage. This dependence on a handful of buyers, several of whom are also building [competing custom silicon](/moat/moats.md), is the structural vulnerability in NVIDIA's otherwise dominant [positioning](/market/positioning.md).
