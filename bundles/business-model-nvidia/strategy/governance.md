# The most valuable company in the world

NVIDIA crossed $4T in 2025, became the first company to reach $5T, and hit a record ~$5.5T on May 13, 2026; by early July 2026 it traded around ~$4.7T (volatile, cite with date). [1][2] FY2026 financials, all verified from NVIDIA's release: revenue $215.9B (+65%), GAAP operating income $130.4B, net income $120.1B. [3] Q1 FY2027 revenue was $81.6B. [4] Those totals are what the [revenue streams](/revenue/revenue-streams.md) roll up to, and what the [capital returns](/economics/cost-structure.md) are paid from.

# Leadership and structure

Co-founder Jensen Huang (the company was founded in 1993) remains chairman and CEO. There is no dual-class structure; a single class of common stock. Founder-CEO continuity is part of why the aggressive [annual-cadence strategy](/strategy/key-bets.md) has been executable.

# China export controls are the standing risk

Export policy is the biggest external variable in the model. The timeline as of mid-2026:

- **April 9, 2025:** a US rule required a license to export the China-market H20 to China. Q1 FY2026 carried a $4.5B charge for unsellable H20; NVIDIA had sold $4.6B of H20 pre-restriction and could not ship ~$2.5B more; Q2 FY2026 guidance absorbed an ~$8B hit. NVIDIA sized the addressable China AI-accelerator market at ~$50B. [5][6]
- **Mid-2025:** a partial reversal permitted H20 sales under a reported arrangement paying 15% of China revenue to the US government.
- **December 2025:** a Huang-Trump agreement aimed to let the H200 re-enter China, but a State Department review reportedly stalled shipments; successor China-compliant parts (B30/B30A) were in play.
- **China was ~9.1% of revenue (~$19.7B) in FY2026**, on a basis NVIDIA shifted to customer-HQ location in Q3 FY2026. [7]

The mid-2026 status of H200-to-China is fluid and unresolved. This exposure is why the [monetization](/revenue/pricing.md) margin story carries a policy asterisk.
